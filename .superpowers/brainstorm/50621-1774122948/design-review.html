Key Design Patterns Identified

BAD / GOOD Toggle

Binary assessment per checklist item. Not dropdowns — simple tap toggle. BAD triggers a notes field.

Inline Camera + Comment

Every inspection item has camera and comment icons inline — no separate photo screen needed.

Category Tabs

Engine | Electrical | Hull | Safety | Nav — horizontal scroll tabs to organize long checklists.

3-Tab Navigation

My Jobs (home) | Service (new service form) | PDI (pre-delivery inspection) — not 4 tabs.

Two Form Types

Service jobs AND Pre-Delivery Inspections are separate workflows with the same checklist UI pattern.

Section Checkboxes

Each section header (Engine Systems, Electrical, etc.) has a checkbox to mark the entire section complete.
