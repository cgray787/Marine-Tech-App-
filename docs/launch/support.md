# Support — JBY-Marine Tech

**Need help with the JBY-Marine Tech app?**

JBY-Marine Tech is an invite-only field-service app for marine technicians. If you don't have an account, contact your shop owner — they provision technician accounts.

---

## Common questions

### I didn't get a TestFlight or invite email.
Ask your shop admin to resend it. Check your spam folder. Invites from `noreply@email.apple.com` (TestFlight) and your shop's address.

### The app says "Account not active."
Your account is either pending approval or has been suspended. Contact your shop admin to activate it.

### I forgot my password.
On the Login screen, tap "Forgot password" — we'll send a reset link to the email associated with your account.

### Photos didn't upload after I submitted a report.
The app shows an alert if any photos failed to upload (usually due to no network at the dock). Re-open the report from the Clients or Jobs tab, re-add the photos, and submit again. The report itself was saved.

### The calendar shows the wrong day.
Make sure your iPhone's date and time are set to **automatic** (Settings → General → Date & Time → Set Automatically).

### I'm offline — what happens?
The app queues your edits locally and syncs them when you're back online. Look for the orange "Offline" banner at the top.
